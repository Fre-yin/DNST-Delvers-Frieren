The Frieren installation packages contain two mod DLLs, twelve runtime PNGs and three sounds per loader. The normal and stressed portraits are original pixel work by the project author depicting Frieren. The Archmage and Booklover icons are edited from native Dungeon Settlers icon sources. The eight Zoltraak images (icon, break icon, magic circle, light impulse, beam, rings, light point and impact) were made for this project from simple original shapes and are not traced from other images. The cast and shot sounds were synthesized for this project. The beam sound is a loop cut from "Spell charge loop" by Bertsz (Freesound 500910, CC0 1.0). No sound or image is cut from the anime. These statements do not claim ownership of the underlying character, designs or original game icons. See THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md for details.

The game, BepInEx and Core are obtained separately. The Frieren package contains no game binary, loader binary, generated interop assembly, Core DLL, source icon template, Aseprite working file, world-hair original or saved campaign.
